About our company

Service expertise that keeps business moving.

Sree Raam Coach Motors is a TATA Authorized Commercial Vehicle Service Station focused on dependable vehicle maintenance, accurate diagnosis and quality repairs across our Perambalur and Pudukottai branches.

Our team supports commercial vehicle owners and fleet operators with a practical, customer-focused approach. We work to identify issues clearly, maintain vehicle performance and help reduce avoidable downtime.

  • Commercial vehicle maintenance and repair
  • Mechanical and electrical fault diagnosis
  • Preventive servicing and periodic maintenance
  • Service support for a wide range of commercial vehicles
