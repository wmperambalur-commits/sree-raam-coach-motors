SREE RAAM COACH MOTORS WEBSITE STARTER
======================================

Files
-----
- index.html: Responsive one-page website starter.
- README.txt: This guide.

Preview
-------
Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox. Internet access is needed for the external vehicle photos.

Before publishing
-----------------
1. Confirm the phone numbers and address displayed on the website.
2. Replace the SR text mark with the official Sree Raam Coach Motors logo.
3. Confirm that the wording "TATA Authorized Commercial Vehicle Service Station" is approved for public use.
4. Confirm the list of services and vehicle categories.
5. Add the actual business hours and a verified Google Maps pin if available.
6. The photos currently load from Unsplash and can be replaced with your own workshop, staff, and vehicle photographs.
7. No customer enquiry form or database is connected in this starter. Call and WhatsApp buttons are active links.

How to publish
--------------
This is a static website and can be hosted by a static hosting provider or regular web hosting.
To use srcmotors.in, first confirm where the domain is registered and whether it is active. Then configure the domain's DNS records to point to the chosen hosting provider. Do not change DNS records until you know whether your company email depends on them. Keep existing MX/TXT email records unless your email provider instructs otherwise.

Important
---------
This package is a website starter, not yet a live website. It does not log in to Squarespace, change your domain, or publish anything automatically. You will need access to your domain/hosting account to publish it.
